description: Open Science at ITC (OS@ITC) is a horizontal facility of the Faculty
  of Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation (ITC) at the University of Twente,
  supporting all ITC departments in adopting Open Science practices. In 2021, it offered
  CODECHECK as a service to ITC researchers, with checks conducted by the faculty's
  Open Science Officer before or after submission of a manuscript. Certificates are
  published in the Open Science at ITC community on Zenodo.
